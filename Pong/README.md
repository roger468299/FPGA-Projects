## Objective
This project will reproduce the Pong game. It will use the push buttons on the Go board to control the paddles. Switches 1 and 2 will belong to player 1 and switches 3 and 4 will belong to player 2.

## Success

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c69545c6-cd89-48dc-923d-84b42b8587fa

The program is working as intended. The game begins when a key is pressed on the keyboard. Before the game begins, the ball is in the middle and switches 1 and 2 control the left paddle and switches 3 and 4 the right paddle.
